ORDERS SHIPPING WITHIN TÜRKİYE

Customers may exercise their legal right of withdrawal within 14 days of receiving their order, without providing a reason, in accordance with applicable consumer protection laws.

Items must be returned in their original condition, without damage or signs of excessive use. Original packaging, labels and included accessories should be preserved where possible.

This right does not apply to products excluded from returns under applicable law. Any such exclusions will be communicated before purchase.

If an item arrives damaged, defective, incorrect or incomplete, please contact our customer care team. VN SHOP will review the issue and arrange an appropriate solution in accordance with applicable consumer protection laws.

Refunds will be processed within the legally required timeframe after the return or withdrawal request has been handled. The time required for the refunded amount to appear in your account may vary depending on your payment provider.

INTERNATIONAL ORDERS

For orders shipped outside Türkiye, return and refund requests are handled in accordance with applicable consumer protection laws in the destination country.

Customers may exercise any applicable statutory withdrawal or return rights within the legally prescribed period.

Items should be returned in their original condition, with all included components and packaging preserved where possible.

Customs duties, import taxes and international return shipping arrangements may vary depending on the destination country. Any applicable costs and responsibilities will be handled in accordance with local law.

For damaged, defective, incorrect or incomplete international orders, please contact our customer care team so we can review and resolve the issue.

DIGITAL MUSIC PURCHASES

Digital music purchases are generally final once digital content has been supplied or access has begun, where permitted by applicable law.

Where required, customers will be asked to provide their express consent to immediate delivery and acknowledge the applicable loss of their right of withdrawal before accessing the digital content.

Nothing in this policy limits any mandatory consumer rights that cannot legally be excluded.

If you experience any issues downloading, accessing or streaming purchased music, please contact our customer care team. We will work to ensure that you receive access to the content you purchased.

DAMAGED, INCORRECT OR INCOMPLETE ORDERS

If your order arrives damaged, defective, incorrect or incomplete, please contact us with your order details.

Our team will review the issue and determine the appropriate solution, which may include a replacement, refund or other remedy in accordance with applicable law.
