1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how VN SHOP — powered by 9ojeez9 and operating within the VN RECORDS and VNION brand ecosystem (“VN SHOP,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you visit our website, use account features, or place an order. It should be read together with our Terms of Use and other applicable store policies.

We aim to collect and use only the information reasonably needed to operate the website, provide requested services, process orders, maintain security, and meet legal obligations.

2. Information We May Collect

  • Account information: your email address and, if you provide them, your first and last name, country, and profile details. Email-based sign-in may use a verification code and an authentication session.
  • Order and customer-service information: products ordered, quantities, selected variants, order status, and details you provide when contacting us about an order, shipping, returns, or support.
  • Delivery and address information: address and contact details you choose to save or provide for an order, where those features are available.
  • Cart and preference information: cart contents and quantities, and cookie preferences saved in your browser so the relevant features can work as expected.
  • Technical information: information that may be provided through normal website requests or service logs, such as browser/device details, timestamps, and network information, where processed by our hosting, authentication, or security providers.
  • Messages you send us: the contents and contact details included when you email us or otherwise request support.

Please do not send payment-card details or other sensitive personal information in ordinary email or support messages.

3. How We Use Information

We may use information, as applicable, to:

  • provide website, account, sign-in, and profile functionality;
  • maintain your shopping cart and support checkout and order fulfilment;
  • manage delivery details, customer support, returns, and order-related communications;
  • remember your cookie choices and website preferences;
  • protect the website, detect misuse, troubleshoot errors, and maintain service security;
  • comply with applicable legal obligations and respond to lawful requests; and
  • send optional updates only where applicable and in accordance with your choices and relevant law.

4. Cookies, Local Storage, and Your Choices

The website may use browser storage, including local storage, to remember shopping-cart contents and cookie preferences and to support account or store functionality. These entries are stored in your browser and may remain there until cleared or overwritten. Essential storage may be necessary for features you request to function.

Use Cookie Choices in the website footer to review and save the available preference settings. The choices shown in the panel describe the options provided by the site; they do not necessarily indicate that every category is active on every page. You can also clear browser storage through your browser settings, although doing so may remove saved cart contents or preferences.

5. Service Providers and Sharing

We may use service providers to host the website, provide account authentication and database functionality, support checkout, or help deliver services you request. Those providers may process information as needed to provide their services, subject to the arrangements and terms applicable to them.

We may also disclose information where required by law, to respond to valid legal process, to protect the rights or safety of users and others, or to address fraud, security, or technical issues. We do not sell personal information for money. Where applicable law gives you additional rights regarding sharing or targeted advertising, you may contact us to make a request.

Links to third-party websites or services are governed by their own privacy notices. We do not control the privacy practices of third parties.

6. Data Retention and Security

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including maintaining an account, completing transactions, providing support, resolving disputes, keeping required business records, and meeting legal obligations. Retention periods may vary according to the type of information and the applicable requirements.

We use reasonable measures intended to protect information handled through the website. No website, transmission, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You may also have the right to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. These rights are subject to applicable exceptions and legal obligations.

To make a privacy request, contact recordsvunion@gmail.com. For your protection, we may need to verify the request before taking action.

8. Children’s Privacy

The website and store are not intended to encourage children to provide personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review the matter and take appropriate steps where required by law.

9. International Processing

Our website and service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where applicable law requires safeguards for international transfers, we will take steps required by that law.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our website, services, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date. Where required by law, we will provide additional notice or seek consent.

11. Contact

For questions or requests about this Privacy Policy or the handling of personal information, contact recordsvunion@gmail.com.
