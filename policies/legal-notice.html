Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

Last updated: October 29, 2026

1. Your Privacy Choices

VN SHOP — powered by 9ojeez9 and operating within the VN RECORDS and VNION brand ecosystem — respects your privacy. This page explains how to submit a request concerning the sale or sharing of personal information and how to exercise applicable privacy rights.

We do not sell personal information for money. Depending on the laws that apply to you, “sale” or “sharing” may have a broader legal meaning than an exchange for money. If you believe your request applies under your local privacy law, you may contact us using the details below.

2. How to Submit a Request

To submit a request to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, or to ask a question about our privacy practices, email recordsvunion@gmail.com with the subject line Privacy Request — Do Not Sell or Share.

Please describe the request and the website or account it concerns. Do not include your password, email verification code, payment-card details, or other sensitive information in your message. We may request reasonable information to verify your identity or authority to make the request before responding.

3. Cookie and Browser Preferences

You can review the available browser preference settings using Cookie Choices in the footer of the website. You may also clear browser storage through your browser settings. Clearing storage can remove saved shopping-cart contents and preferences, and does not by itself submit a formal privacy-rights request.

4. Authorized Agents and Non-Discrimination

Where applicable law permits an authorized agent to submit a request on your behalf, we may ask for evidence of authorization and may need to verify your identity directly. We will not unlawfully discriminate against you for exercising a privacy right protected by applicable law.

5. Other Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may also have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. For more information about the information we handle and how it is used, see our Privacy Policy.

6. Contact

Send privacy requests and questions to recordsvunion@gmail.com. We will review requests and respond in accordance with applicable law.
